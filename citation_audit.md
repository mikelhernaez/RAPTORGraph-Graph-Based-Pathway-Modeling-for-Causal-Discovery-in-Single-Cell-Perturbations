## 4. Recommended theorem additions (identifiability section)

Theoretical results for the manuscript's "Identifiability from targeted interventions" section and its supplement. All statements are **paraphrased** from the published sources (not verbatim), attributed to original authors, and are established results — **not** new theorems proved for RAPTORGraph.

> **Architecture note:** RAPTORGraph is a **β-VAE** (reparameterization trick + KL-divergence loss with a β-weight; see `Exogeneous latent factor inference via VAE`, main.tex ~977–978, 1030). The comparison baselines are comparable in kind: **dVAE (Zhang et al.)** and **SENA** describe their models as VAEs, but their training uses a β-weighted KL term, i.e. effectively a β-VAE. (Phrase carefully — the papers call themselves VAEs; calling them β-VAEs is our characterization of their objective.) This determines which identifiability results are load-bearing: VAE-relevant results (Zhang, Khemakhem) are core; **deterministic**-autoencoder results (Ahuja) are **not** applicable.

### Key attribution results

- **Zhang et al. (2023)** — affine identifiability of latent dim (Lemma 1); ancestral identifiability (Thm 1); graph identifiability up to causal-disentanglement equivalence (Thm 2); prediction of unseen intervention combinations (Thm 3). Setting: unpaired single-target **soft** interventions covering every latent variable. Note: graph identifiability does **not** imply component-wise identifiability of latents. **Core — this is RAPTORGraph's cornerstone.**
- **Khemakhem et al. (2020)** — identifiability up to linear transform of sufficient statistics (Thm 1); component-wise under extra conditions (Thm 2). **Core for a VAE:** RAPTORGraph is a VAE, so this justifies identifiability of the latent representation despite the variational encoder. Caveat: the guarantee requires a specific conditional latent model + auxiliary variable, which RAPTORGraph's setup only partially matches — state this honestly.
- **DAGMA (Bello et al. 2022)** — log-determinant acyclicity characterization: `h_ldet^s(W)=0 ⟺ W is a DAG`. Establishes **acyclicity**, not uniqueness of the recovered graph — keep as a separate theoretical claim from identifiability. **Core (mechanism).**
- **Varıcı et al. (2024)** — unknown **multi-node** interventions; diversity of the intervention-target matrix (full row rank) enables identifiability up to ancestors (soft) and perfect latent recovery (hard). Shows single-target interventions are not universally necessary — frames *why* the single-node fix matters. **Contrast, not load-bearing.**
- **Ahuja et al. (2023)** — affine identification by a **deterministic** autoencoder (Thm 4.4); identification from **fixed-value** interventions (Thm 5.3). **Not applicable to RAPTORGraph:** it is a VAE, not a deterministic autoencoder, and fixed-value interventions are not the soft-intervention setting. Keep only as a related-work mention at most.
- **Classical graph identifiability** — LiNGAM (Shimizu et al. 2006, non-Gaussian); restricted additive-noise models (Peters et al. 2014, note "restricted"); equal-variance Gaussian (Peters & Bühlmann 2014). **Optional** — only if discussing graph ID independent of the latent model.

### Caveats for the manuscript

- These are population-level, idealized results under specific assumptions on the observation map and interventional distributions. They do **not** prove an arbitrary neural encoder / sparse pathway layer / learned acyclic graph inherits the guarantees.
- A one-to-one mapping from targeted genes to pathway entry points, even with an acyclic pathway graph, is **insufficient** to prove the latent pathways or graph are uniquely recoverable. That requires a separate theorem tying RAPTORGraph's actual encoder, structural equations, intervention semantics, and decoder to suitable identification assumptions.
- Zhang's faithfulness assumption as stated in the collection is a summary; a formal proof invoking their theorem must impose the full original condition. The Ahuja citation in that assumption should be removed when stating Zhang's faithfulness condition specifically (the two assumptions are not identical).
- Khemakhem's guarantee requires a conditional latent model + auxiliary variable, which RAPTORGraph's β-VAE only partially matches — do not overclaim that the VAE architecture alone inherits it.
